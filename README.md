## About me

I am Mauricio, a machine learning scientist and AI engineer. I hold a PhD in Biomedical Engineering and Imaging Sciences from King's College London (Marie Skłodowska-Curie fellow), for my work on domain adaptation for medical image segmentation. I have worked in machine learning for more than nine years across research and industry, building computer vision and anomaly detection systems for teledermatology (OMHU) and decentralised clinical trials (Medable).

Currently I work on:

- **LLM systems for healthcare**: structured data extraction from clinical records, with de-identification, schema validation and systematic evaluation.
- **Foundation models for medical image segmentation**: interactive 3D segmentation models that detect and correct their own errors (Visiting Researcher, King's College London). Earlier work: [MAIS: Memory-Attention for Interactive Segmentation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.07511) (MIDL 2025).

Publications on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=DjAfOigAAAAJ) · Connect on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mauricio-orbes)
